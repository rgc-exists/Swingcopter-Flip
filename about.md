# Swingcopter Flip
A mod that flips the player's visuals upside down when gravity is reversed in swingcopter mode. Makes it easier to keep track of what your gravity is.

# Why this *SHOULD* be added to the vanilla game
## The problem
Ever since 2.2's release the Swing gamemode has been frowned upon by many when it comes to hard challenges and levels.  
Many extreme demon creators have opted to stick with the old 2.1 Swingcopter design.
## Why is this?
The primary reason the swing is so bad to use for hard gameplay is because of how slowly it changes its velocity.  
You can press the button to swap gravity, but if you're already facing downwards it takes a second to even start moving upwards at all.  
# The solution
**This mod argues that this could be at least partially fixed by adjusting the swing visually when the gravity is swapped.**
# Arguments against the idea
In the past when I brought up this idea many people pointed out that the swing gamemode DOES technically have a visual indicator for gravity, that being the fire coming out of the boosters in the back.  
HOWEVER, I would argue that the idea of looking back and forth at that very specific spot on your screen just to see what gravity your swing is is not reasonable when you're trying to actually play the game. For this to work you need to be able to see the indicator out of the corner of your eye and still tell immediately which direction it is.

# SPREAD THE WORD!
This would be VERY useful as an official feature in vanilla Geometry Dash.  
Some swings, particularly symetrical ones with circles on the top like the default, need to be flipped when right side up to look like they're "facing downard", but RobTop can just do what I did and hard-code the orientation per swing icon, as well as add a setting to reverse the hard-coded value.

# "Flip Sprite When Right Side Up" Setting
**This is a setting that exists because some swingcopter icons face up so it can feel confusing without it.**
*By default it automatically adjusts based on what I (the developer) thought looked best for the icon you have selected. You can override it in the mod settings.*
